## MOLA global DEM, read 2026-11-16, outside Git

The far skyline (Mount Sharp) comes from the MGS MOLA DEM mosaic, 463 m a pixel, read as a window over HTTP, never downloaded whole.
[Product page](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_mgs_mola_dem_463m) (USGS Astrogeology): access constraints "CC0 (public domain)". Credit:
NASA/GSFC/MOLA Science Team, USGS. Not legal advice. The `.r16` made from it is under `unreal/Saved/`, outside Git; the report is
[`far.json`](results/terrain-gale/far.json). Measured and interpolated parts are told apart in [the terrain results](results/terrain-gale/README.md#mount-sharp-in-the-distance-mola-2026-11-16).

## Poly Haven assets, downloaded 2026-11-16, outside Git

**Not in Git.** Files are in `unreal/Content/Licensed/PolyHaven/`, which `.gitignore` excludes, and the Unreal assets made from
them stay under `unreal/Content/Licensed/` too. Fetched by [`scripts/assets/polyhaven_fetch.py`](../scripts/assets/polyhaven_fetch.py)
from the public API, 28 files, 57.5 MB, each checked against the API's md5 (`downloaded.json` lists URLs and hashes).

**Terms read on 2026-11-16, not legal advice:**
[license page](https://polyhaven.com/license) says the assets are CC0 and no credit is needed; the
[API Terms of Service](https://github.com/Poly-Haven/Public-API/blob/master/ToS.md) allow API use for any purpose if calls carry a
User-Agent that names the software (`Roverloom`), and ask for a credit only where the live API surfaces content in a product. The
site's terms prohibit scraping, so the list is fixed and short. Credit is given anyway: "Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), CC0".

| Asset | Page | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `dry_ground_01` (2k: diffuse, GL normal, ARM) | https://polyhaven.com/a/dry_ground_01 | Landscape layer: regolith |
| `dense_sand` (2k) | https://polyhaven.com/a/dense_sand | Landscape layer: sand |
| `rock_boulder_cracked` (2k) | https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_cracked | Landscape layer: bedrock |
| `rocks_ground_03` (2k) | https://polyhaven.com/a/rocks_ground_03 | Landscape layer: gravel |
| `namaqualand_boulder_02`, `_04`, `_06`, `rock_09` (1k FBX with textures) | https://polyhaven.com/a/namaqualand_boulder_02 and the others by id | Scattered rocks; solid in races above 15 cm radius (`build_rock_collision.py`) |
| `namaqualand_boulder_03`, `_05`, `rock_07`, `stone_01`, `moon_rock_01`, `moon_rock_03` (1k FBX with textures), added 2026-11-27 | https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_07 and the others by id | Extra shapes for small stones only (under 12 cm), never solid |

These are Earth rocks and soil used as stand-ins for Martian surfaces. They are not Martian materials and their look says
nothing about regolith properties. No Poly Haven HDRI is used: the sky is Unreal's Sky Atmosphere, tuned by hand.

## ambientCG materials, downloaded 2026-11-27, outside Git

[`scripts/assets/ambientcg_fetch.py`](../scripts/assets/ambientcg_fetch.py) downloads a fixed list of 2K-JPG materials
into `unreal/Content/Licensed/AmbientCG/`, which Git ignores, and packs each material's ambient occlusion and roughness
into one ARM map, as Poly Haven's are. License, read 2026-11-27 at https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/: "All ambientCG
assets are provided under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal License", including in a video game; credit is not
required. Credit is given anyway: "Created using <asset> from ambientCG.com, licensed under CC0 1.0".

| Asset | Source | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Ground079S` | https://ambientcg.com/a/Ground079S | Landscape regolith (Gale, Noctis); gravel (Amazonis) |
| `Ground098` | https://ambientcg.com/a/Ground098 | Landscape sand: wind-rippled drifts (Gale, Noctis, Amazonis) |
| `Ground099` | https://ambientcg.com/a/Ground099 | Landscape regolith: fine dust (Amazonis) |
| `Ground110` | https://ambientcg.com/a/Ground110 | Landscape gravel: dark grit (Gale, Noctis) |
| `Rock029` | https://ambientcg.com/a/Rock029 | Landscape bedrock: layered red-orange rock (Gale) |
| `Rock061` | https://ambientcg.com/a/Rock061 | Landscape bedrock: layered brown rock (Noctis) |
| `Rock062` | https://ambientcg.com/a/Rock062 | Landscape bedrock: cracked grey rock (Amazonis) |

Chosen by eye from the previews; like the Poly Haven files, they are Earth materials standing in for Martian ground.
`build_gale_material.py` names them per site (`layers` in `LOOKS`) and falls back to the Poly Haven layer for any that
was not imported. Lyot and the polar dunes keep the Poly Haven layers under their frost.

**HiRISE-derived heightfields, outside Git** (source: the HiRISE DTM above, CC0-1.0, credit "NASA/JPL/University of Arizona"; DTM distributed
by USGS): `gale_site.r16` (1 km, 1 m posts) and `gale_horizon.r16` (5.6 km, 5.55 m posts, 18.2% interpolated), made by
[`hirise_site.py`](../scripts/terrain/hirise_site.py) and [`hirise_horizon.py`](../scripts/terrain/hirise_horizon.py), in
`unreal/Saved/Terrain/gale/`. A copy of `gale_site.r16` is in `scenarios/terrains/data/`, which `.gitignore` excludes for `*.r16`. The Unreal
Landscape, level, and materials made from them and from the Poly Haven files are in `unreal/Content/Licensed/Gale/`, also ignored.
